Partner Privacy Notice

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This notice is for Blio partners (delivery partners and fish cleaning and packing helpers). Blio is a brand of Farmblue Agrotech Private Limited ("we", "us"), House No 1328, A/p Harkul, Khalachi Teliwadi, Kankavli, Sindhudurg 416602, Maharashtra. It explains what personal data we keep about you, why, and your choices. We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Customers' data is covered separately in our Privacy Policy.

What we keep

  • Your name, mobile number and area, to offer you shifts and contact you.
  • Your UPI ID, to pay you.
  • Your PIN for the Blio Partner app.
  • Your work record: dates, number of deliveries, route km, kg cleaned, pay, whether it is paid, and ratings.
  • A record that we checked your documents: we look at your driving licence and vehicle papers (and, for cleaning helpers, your food-handler health check), through DigiLocker or the originals. We note only the date and who checked. We do not keep copies or numbers of your licence, papers or other IDs.
  • A record that you agreed to the Partner Terms and this notice, with the date.
  • Your WhatsApp messages to us and in the partner group.

Why we use it

Only to offer and share out shifts, pay you, check that partners are safe and hygienic, keep the accounting records the law requires, and handle questions and complaints. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for marketing.

Who else handles it

  • Google: your details and work record are stored in a Google Sheet in our company Google account, through Google Apps Script. Google Fonts also loads this site's fonts.
  • WhatsApp (Meta), where partner messages and the partner group run.
  • Cloudflare, which hosts this site and the app.
  • Customers may see your first name and phone number when you deliver, so they can reach you about their order.
  • Our accountant, who sees payment records for our accounts and taxes.

On your phone

The Blio Partner app saves your mobile number and PIN in your phone's browser storage so you stay signed in. Tap "Sign out on this phone" to remove them, especially on a shared phone.

Customer data you see

For a delivery you see the customer's name, number and address. That is their personal data too. Use it only for that delivery and do not keep or share it. Details are in the Partner Terms.

How long we keep it

We keep payment records for as long as accounting and tax law require. Other details, such as your contact details and ratings, are kept while you are an active partner. After you stop, we delete them unless we must keep them.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can withdraw your agreement at any time (then we stop offering you shifts). Write to connect@farmblue.in or WhatsApp +91 88568 88766. We will delete what we are not legally required to keep, and tell you what we must keep and why.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, contact our grievance officer: Malhar Pansare, Grievance Officer, Farmblue Agrotech Private Limited. WhatsApp or call +91 88568 88766, or email connect@farmblue.in. We acknowledge every complaint within 48 hours and resolve it within one month.

Changes

If we change this notice, we will update the date above.
