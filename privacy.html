Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Blio is a brand of Farmblue Agrotech Private Limited ("we", "us"), House No 1328, A/p Harkul, Khalachi Teliwadi, Kankavli, Sindhudurg 416602, Maharashtra. This page explains what personal data we collect when you order fish from Blio, why we collect it, and your choices. We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Your name and mobile number, so we can confirm your order and reach you about delivery.
  • Your delivery address and pinned map location, so the delivery person can find you.
  • Your order details: what you ordered, quantity, cut, price, bill and delivery date.
  • Messages you send us on WhatsApp, including photos if you report a problem.

We do not collect your card, bank or UPI PIN details. You pay through your own UPI app after we confirm your bill.

Why we use it

Only to take, confirm, pack, deliver and bill your order, to check discount coupons, to handle complaints and refunds, and to keep the accounting records the law requires. We do not sell your data. We do not send marketing messages unless you ask us to.

On your phone

To show "My orders", this site saves your order numbers and details in your browser's storage on your own phone. Clearing your browser data removes them from your phone.

Who else handles it

  • Google: when you place an order it is saved to a Google Sheet in our company Google account, through Google Apps Script. Google Fonts also loads this site's fonts.
  • WhatsApp (Meta), where order messages and chats reach us.
  • Cloudflare, which hosts this site and gives us basic visit counts without cookies.
  • OpenStreetMap and cdnjs: when you open the map to pin your location, map tiles and the map software load from them, so they can see your IP address.
  • Our delivery person, who sees your name, number and address for your delivery only.

How long we keep it

We keep order and bill records for as long as accounting and tax law require. Other details, such as chat messages, are kept only as long as needed to serve you.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Write to connect@farmblue.in or WhatsApp +91 88568 88766. We will delete what we are not legally required to keep, and tell you what we must keep and why.

Children

Blio is not meant for children under 18. Please order through an adult.

Complaints

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above.
