Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Fish is perishable and is often cleaned and cut to your choice, so we cannot take it back. We will always make it right if something is wrong with your order.

If something is wrong

Message us on WhatsApp at +91 88568 88766 within 2 hours of delivery, with your order number and a clear photo, if:

  • the fish is not fresh or is damaged,
  • an item is missing or is not what you ordered, or
  • the weight is clearly short of what we billed.

After we check, we will, as you prefer, replace the item with the next delivery, or refund the amount for that item.

Cancelling an order

  • Before we confirm your bill: cancel any time, at no cost.
  • After you have paid and before we start cleaning your fish: we will refund in full.
  • After cleaning or cutting has begun: we cannot cancel, because the fish is already prepared for you.

If we cannot deliver

If we cannot deliver your order because stock runs out or the catch does not arrive, we will tell you at once and refund anything you have paid.

When not covered

We cannot refund for a change of mind after delivery, for fish that was not kept cold or cooked in time, or for the normal loss in weight from cleaning and cutting, which is explained in our Terms.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the UPI account you paid from within 5 working days of us approving them.
