Partner Terms

Last updated: 13 November 2026

These terms are between you and Farmblue Agrotech Private Limited ("Blio", "we", "us"), House No 1328, A/p Harkul, Khalachi Teliwadi, Kankavli, Sindhudurg 416602, Maharashtra. They apply to people who do part-time delivery or fish cleaning and packing work for Blio ("partners"). Please read them before you start. If anything is unclear, ask us on WhatsApp and we will explain it in Marathi or Hindi.

How partner work is arranged

Partner work is part-time and flexible. There are no fixed hours. We post open requests for evenings or sessions in the Blio Partner app and in the partner WhatsApp group, and you choose which ones to take. You may say no to any request. Taking a shift is a commitment to that shift, so please tell us as early as you can if you cannot make it. In the app you can give up a place before the day of the shift.

Shifts are shared fairly

Requests are first come, first served. So that everyone gets a chance, a partner who has worked the last two shift days may not be offered the next one. We may offer it anyway if we need more people.

Pay

  • Delivery partners: a fixed amount per evening, an amount for each delivery, and fuel for the full route, worked out from the map.
  • Fish cleaning and packing helpers: a fixed amount per session plus an amount per kg of fish, and per kg of prawns and crab, cleaned.

The current rates are on the Work with Blio page and in the Blio Partner app. Rates are tentative and we can change them. We tell you before a change, and a change applies only to work done after it. After each evening or session we record what you did and what you earned. You can see it in the app as Due until we pay it by UPI, then as Paid. If something looks wrong, tell us on WhatsApp and we will check it against our record and correct any mistake.

Any bonus for top-rated partners is decided by us and announced in advance. Pay is for work done; we do not deduct from it without telling you why.

Cash from customers

Some customers pay the delivery person in cash. If you collect cash, you hand over the full amount to Blio the same evening, and we match it against the orders. Do not take payment for anything that is not on your delivery list.

Your vehicle, licence and papers

Delivery partners use their own vehicle and must have a valid driving licence and vehicle papers. We check them once, when you start, and again if they expire. You are responsible for following traffic laws and for riding safely. Blio does not provide a vehicle. Fuel is paid as described above.

Food safety and hygiene

Fish is perishable. Wash your hands before handling packs, keep packs sealed and cold, and never open a customer's pack. Cleaning helpers wear the gloves and aprons we provide, follow the cleaning room rules, and need a food-handler health check. Do not work if you are unwell. Tell us straight away if you see anything that could make food unsafe.

Conduct and safety

  • Treat customers, other partners and our team with respect. Harassment, abuse or discrimination of any kind is not acceptable.
  • Do not work under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
  • Stop and call us if a delivery feels unsafe. You never need to enter a place you are not comfortable entering.
  • Do not hand your work to someone else. If you cannot do a shift, give it up so we can offer it to another partner.

Customer information

For a delivery you will see a customer's name, number and address. Use them only to deliver that order. Do not save, share, post or use them for anything else, and do not contact customers about anything other than their delivery. See the Partner Privacy Notice.

Ratings

Customers and our team may rate your work. Ratings help us decide who to offer shifts to and who earns the monthly bonus. If you think a rating is unfair, tell us and we will look at it.

Pausing and stopping

You can stop any time by telling us. We can pause or end a partner account, for example for safety, hygiene or conduct reasons, and we will tell you why. We will pay whatever is due for work already done.

Complaints

Our grievance officer is Malhar Pansare, Grievance Officer, Farmblue Agrotech Private Limited. WhatsApp or call +91 88568 88766, or email connect@farmblue.in. We acknowledge every complaint within 48 hours and resolve it within one month.

Changes

If we change these terms, we will update the date above and tell partners in the partner group.
